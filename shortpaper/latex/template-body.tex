\section{Introduction}

State the problem, the evidence gap, and two or three contributions.

\section{Method or Diagnostic Framework}

Define all observable states, labels, and assumptions before presenting an intervention.

\section{Experiments}

Describe datasets, baselines, metrics, uncertainty, and reproducibility details. All figures, tables, and appendices count toward the four-page main-text limit.

\section{Limitations}

Separate verified findings from scope restrictions and unresolved validity threats.

\section{Conclusion}

Summarize only claims supported by the reported evidence.
